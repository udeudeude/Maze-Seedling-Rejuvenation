PHYSICAL → DIGITAL

Photograph your maze

Photograph your wooden board from directly overhead, with even lighting. The scanner suggests wall locations; you review its guesses before applying them.

Photographs remain on your phone. Wood/floor color sampling and four-corner alignment are approximate, especially under shadows. Start, exit, and traps are placed manually after scanning.

TWO DEVICES · ONE LABYRINTH

Remote control

Easy pairing (internet required)

Open this page on both devices. On the phone in Cardboard tap Host. On the controller enter the host's short code and tap Connect. Easy pairing uses a public PeerJS signaling service; the actual controls travel over WebRTC.

Not connected
Advanced: manual peer-to-peer pairing (no signaling service)

On the VR device choose Create offer, then copy it into the other device's box and choose Accept offer. Copy the resulting answer back and choose Apply answer.

How it works

Build an 18×18 digital representation of your physical maze, or photograph it and use the scan assistant to suggest walls. Imagine the setting and add encounters. Explore on the screen or in a Cardboard headset.

Save to your device and export JSON for backup. Web browsers cannot recreate the retired iOS application's exact computer vision or historical assets; this is a new independent implementation.
