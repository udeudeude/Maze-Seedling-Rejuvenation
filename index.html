Second-device controller

Use these buttons on the same phone, or pair another device using the experimental manual WebRTC connection below.

Peer-to-peer pairing

On the VR phone choose Host, copy its offer to the controller phone, and paste it under Join. Then copy the answer back to the host. No signaling server required. Both devices must have network access for connection establishment.
